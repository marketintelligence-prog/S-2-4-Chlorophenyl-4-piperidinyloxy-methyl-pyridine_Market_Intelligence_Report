Structure and (S)-configuration check

Stereocentre C* carries four different groups. CIP priorities:

  1. O — piperidin-4-yloxy (highest atomic number)
  2. C-2 of pyridine — ring N appears in its first substituent sphere
  3. C-1 of 4-chlorophenyl — carbon-only first sphere (Cl is three bonds away)
  4. H — implicit, pointing behind the plane (opposite the wedged C–O bond)

Viewed with H to the rear, 1 → 2 → 3 runs counter-clockwise ⇒ (S). This matches the GSRS isomeric SMILES ([C@H]) and the InChI stereo layer /t17-/m0/s1 22FDA GSRS.

ℹ
Why the stereocentre matters commerciallyThe configuration set here is carried unchanged into bepotastine, (+)-(S)-4-{4-[(4-chlorophenyl)(2-pyridyl)methoxy]piperidino}butanoic acid 13CA 2687445. Enantiomeric purity is therefore a release-critical attribute for every buyer; the (R)-enantiomer becomes the API's chiral impurity.

What it is and why it matters. The molecule is a diaryl-methyl ether: a 4-chlorophenyl ring and a pyridin-2-yl ring meet at one stereogenic carbon, which is etherified to the 4-position of piperidine. The piperidine nitrogen is left free (secondary amine, predicted pKa ≈ 9.7) 23ChemicalBook so that it can be N-alkylated with ethyl 4-bromobutyrate — the step that turns this compound into the bepotastine skeleton 1DrugFuture. Because the chiral centre is fixed (normally by diastereomeric-salt resolution) at this stage, it is the last and most value-dense chiral intermediate before the API; Indian API makers import it and perform only the final N-alkylation, hydrolysis and besilate salt formation 9Trade data2US 7,282,589.

India strategic noteIndia imports the already-resolved (S)-intermediate and adds only the last two to three GMP steps (N-alkylation, hydrolysis, besilate salt formation). Why not "Recommended": the observed Indian market is only ≈0.13 MT/yr (≈US$ 0.12 MN/yr), controlled by one supplier (97.1%) and one buyer (74.2%); annualised, that is ≈US$ 117k of imports a year, so even a 50% share at a 12–15% trading margin would yield only ≈US$ 7–9k of gross margin 9Trade data.
